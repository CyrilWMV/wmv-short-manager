Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. About WMV Short Manager

WMV Short Manager is a desktop application designed to help content creators organize, manage, and schedule video uploads to platforms such as YouTube and TikTok.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, the application may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, video files, captions, descriptions, scheduling information, and publication statuses.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to authenticate users, manage video uploads, prepare scheduled publications, and track publishing results.

4. Third-Party Services

WMV Short Manager may connect to YouTube and TikTok through their official APIs and authorization systems. These services process information according to their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

The application is designed for local desktop use. Application data and authentication credentials may be stored locally to support its features. Appropriate safeguards should be used to protect this information against unauthorized access.

6. Data Sharing

Information required for publishing may be transmitted to the selected social media platform. WMV Short Manager is not intended to sell users' personal information.

7. User Control and Data Deletion

Users can revoke application access through their connected platform's account settings. Requests concerning locally stored application data can be submitted through the project's GitHub repository.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application develops or its features change.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the developer through the WMV Short Manager GitHub repository's Issues section.
